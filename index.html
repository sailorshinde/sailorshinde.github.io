How I approach the first 90 days

The priorities depend on the business. This is how I establish them.

  1. Days 1–30

    Find out what is actually true.

    Understand where leads come from, which channels convert, what the stack actually does, and where sales and marketing need a shared view of the numbers. Talk with sales, BDR, and RevOps leadership, then deliver a written readout of the findings and priorities.

  2. Days 31–60

    Fix the highest-value bottlenecks.

    Pick the two bottlenecks with the biggest commercial impact. Address the measurement gaps that make decisions unreliable—whether in lead scoring, lifecycle routing, or attribution. Then ship one automation that removes real hours from the team’s week.

  3. Days 61–90

    Make the progress repeatable.

    Establish a weekly pipeline review the team can run without me and a quarterly report the CMO can confidently take to the board. Put one AI workflow into marketers’ hands, with the training and ownership to use it without needing me in the room.

How I work

I own the number, not just the channel. I build systems marketers can run without me. I do not buy a tool without a business case. And I would rather change the investment than defend a channel that is not delivering.
