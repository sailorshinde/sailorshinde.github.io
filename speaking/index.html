Speaking

The start line isn’t the whole story.

I’m Gaurav Shinde—an offshore sailor and marketing leader. I speak about ambition, preparation, difficult decisions, and what happens when the plan you have spent years building comes apart.

Invite me to speakFollow the sailing

Years of preparation. One difficult passage home.

I started sailing at 11 with the Sea Cadet Corps. I went on to race in the Clipper Round the World Race and become the first civilian to receive the Admiral Ramdas Trophy for offshore sailing.

For the 2022 Golden Globe Race, I prepared Good Hope, a Baba 35, myself. Years of work went into a campaign that ended before the start: a shoulder injury on the passage there forced me to turn back.

I sailed home from the middle of the Atlantic with a broken shoulder. That passage is as much a part of my story as any race.

What I speak about

  1. 01

    Ambition before certainty

    You do not get certainty before committing to something difficult. Starting small, learning the craft, and doing the preparation are part of the ambition—not a prelude to it.

  2. 02

    Decisions when the plan breaks

    What happens when continuing is no longer the right decision? The injury, the missed start, and the passage home are a story about judgement, not just endurance.

  3. 03

    Doing the work

    Preparing Good Hope meant taking responsibility for the work myself. A first-hand account of the unglamorous preparation behind an ambitious undertaking.
